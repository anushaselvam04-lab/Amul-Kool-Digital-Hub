KOOL MIXOLOGY

More ways to
enjoy your Kool.

Level up your dessert table with these quick, refreshing culinary creations crafted with Amul Kool Elaichi.

01 5 Mins Prep

Royal Elaichi Falooda Shake

Blend 1 bottle chilled Amul Kool Elaichi with soaked sabja seeds, rose vermicelli, and a scoop of Amul Vanilla Ice Cream.

02 Overnight Freeze

Artisanal Elaichi Matka Kulfi

Simmer Amul Kool Elaichi with condensed milk, crushed pistachios, and saffron. Pour into earthen pots and freeze.

03 Breakfast Bowl

Cardamom Chia Superfood Bowl

Soak 3 tablespoons chia seeds in Amul Kool Elaichi for 2 hours. Top with toasted almonds, chia seeds, and pomegranate arils.
